# CBDC Research Pilot - Executive Summary for Regulators

## Executive Overview

The CBDC Research Pilot represents a comprehensive, production-ready simulation framework designed for serious economic research and policy analysis of Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) systems. This executive summary provides regulators with a high-level overview of the system's capabilities, compliance features, and research applications.

## System Overview

### Purpose and Scope
The CBDC Research Pilot is designed to:
- **Simulate realistic CBDC ecosystems** with diverse user types and transaction patterns
- **Analyze economic impact** of CBDC implementation on monetary policy and financial stability
- **Test regulatory compliance** frameworks including AML/KYC, sanctions screening, and risk management
- **Evaluate system performance** under various economic conditions and stress scenarios
- **Generate research data** for policy analysis and regulatory decision-making

### Key Capabilities
- **Multi-user ecosystem simulation** with 7 distinct user types (individuals, businesses, banks, merchants, government, central bank, financial institutions)
- **Realistic transaction generation** with configurable economic parameters and behavioral models
- **Comprehensive compliance framework** aligned with BIS, IMF, and FATF standards
- **Privacy-preserving analytics** using differential privacy and data anonymization techniques
- **Immutable audit trail** with cryptographic verification for regulatory compliance
- **Real-time monitoring dashboard** for compliance and performance tracking
